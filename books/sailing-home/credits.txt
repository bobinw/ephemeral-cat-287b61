Sailing Home
Basava and his sister Sundari live in the port-town of Mamallapuram
during the Pallava period. Their father, a sailor, has not returned
from his voyage to Kamboja and the children are worried. When they
go to the port to find out if anyone there has information about their
father's ship, they realise to their dismay that the foreign sailors
there don't speak a word of their language! What are they going to
do now?
