The Story of Stories
In a happy, happy, happy forest lives Journalist Bear who is sad because
he can't find a 'BREAKING NEWS' story. A Journalist Bear without a
'BREAKING NEWS' story, can you imagine! Then along comes Singing
Sardarji who helps Bear see who he really is. Read this story which
celebrates the power and magic of stories, and the people who weave
them into being.
Pratham Books goes digital to weave a whole new chapter in the realm of multilingual children's stories. Knitting together children, authors, illustrators and
publishers. Folding in teachers, and translators. To create a rich fabric of openly licensed multilingual stories for the children of India 
​​
 and the world. Our unique
online platform, StoryWeaver, is a playground where children, parents, teachers and librarians can get creative. Come, start weaving today, and help us get a
