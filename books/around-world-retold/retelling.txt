## A Very Quiet Life|十分安静的生活
Phileas Fogg lived in London, where every part of his day seemed to follow the hands of a clock.|菲利亚斯·福格住在伦敦，他一天中的每件事似乎都跟着钟表指针进行。
He ate at fixed times, walked at a steady pace, and rarely showed surprise.|他定时吃饭，以稳定的步伐行走，很少表现出惊讶。
His new servant, a cheerful Frenchman called Passepartout, hoped that working for him would mean a peaceful life.|他的新仆人是个开朗的法国人，名叫路路通，希望这份工作能带来平静的生活。
Passepartout had been a performer and a fireman, and he was ready for a rest from excitement.|路路通做过表演者和消防员，已经准备远离刺激，好好休息。
On his first day, he carefully checked the house while Fogg went to his club as usual.|上工第一天，他仔细检查屋子，福格则照常去了俱乐部。
Everything seemed wonderfully regular, from the warm rooms to the times written in his instructions.|一切都井然有序，从温暖的房间，到工作说明上写好的时间，都令人满意。
But before that day ended, the quiet household would be preparing for a journey around the world.|可这一天尚未结束，这个安静的家就要为一场环球旅行作准备了。
## The Eighty-Day Challenge|八十天的挑战
At the club, a newspaper report led Fogg and several other men to discuss how quickly a traveler could circle the earth.|在俱乐部，一则报纸消息让福格和几位先生讨论起：旅行者最快能用多久绕地球一圈。
New railways, regular steamships, and the route through the Suez Canal made the world seem smaller.|新的铁路、定期航行的轮船，以及穿过苏伊士运河的航线，让世界显得更小了。
The published plan allowed eighty days, but the others pointed out storms, accidents, and missed connections.|报上的计划需要八十天，但其他人指出，途中可能有风暴、事故和错过的班次。
Fogg declared that he could do it, and he made a wager of twenty thousand pounds.|福格宣布自己能够做到，并押上了两万英镑的赌注。
He had to return to the club by a quarter to nine on the evening of December twenty-first.|他必须在十二月二十一日晚上八点四十五分之前回到俱乐部。
That very evening, he told an astonished Passepartout to pack a small bag and come with him.|就在当天晚上，他让惊讶不已的路路通收拾一个小旅行袋，跟自己出发。
Their train left London, and the life Passepartout had expected disappeared behind them.|火车驶离伦敦，路路通原本期待的生活，就这样被留在了身后。
## A Detective at Suez|苏伊士的侦探
The travelers crossed France and Italy, then boarded a steamship that carried them toward Suez.|旅行者穿过法国和意大利，登上轮船，驶向苏伊士。
As the ship passed along its route, a detective named Fix watched the passengers with special interest.|轮船沿途航行时，一位名叫费克斯的侦探格外留意着乘客。
A large sum of money had been stolen from a London bank, and Fix thought Fogg resembled the wanted man.|伦敦一家银行有一大笔钱被盗，费克斯认为福格很像通缉中的人。
Fogg's money and sudden departure seemed suspicious to him, although they did not prove a crime.|福格带着大笔钱，又突然出发，在他看来很可疑，尽管这些并不能证明犯罪。
Fix waited for an arrest warrant and questioned Passepartout, who happily talked about their remarkable journey.|费克斯等待逮捕令，又向路路通打听情况；路路通高兴地讲起这趟非凡的旅程。
The servant did not understand why this friendly stranger wanted so many details.|仆人不明白，这位友善的陌生人为什么想知道那么多细节。
When the ship sailed onward to Bombay, Fix followed, carrying his suspicion with him.|轮船继续驶向孟买时，费克斯也跟了上去，心中仍带着怀疑。
## Where the Railway Stops|铁路尽头
In India, Fogg and Passepartout took a train eastward, expecting the railway to carry them toward Calcutta.|到了印度，福格和路路通坐上东行列车，指望铁路将他们带往加尔各答。
Then the train stopped, and they discovered that a section of the line had not yet been completed.|可列车停了下来，他们发现其中一段铁轨尚未修好。
The timetable had made the journey look simple, but the ground before them told a different story.|时刻表让旅程看起来很简单，眼前的实际情况却完全不同。
Fogg did not spend long complaining; he looked for another way to reach the next part of the railway.|福格没有长时间抱怨，而是寻找别的办法，前往下一段铁路。
He bought an elephant and hired a guide who knew the paths through the country.|他买下一头大象，又雇了一位熟悉当地道路的向导。
With a fellow traveler, they continued through fields and forest, moving more slowly than a train but still moving east.|他们与一位同行旅客穿过田野和森林，虽然不及火车快，却仍在向东前进。
Passepartout discovered that a plan could be exact on paper and still need changing in real life.|路路通发现，纸上的计划即使十分精确，到了实际生活中仍可能需要改变。
## Time for Another Person|为另一个人停下
In the forest, the guide told them that a young widow named Aouda was being taken to a ceremony that would end her life.|森林中，向导告诉他们，一位名叫艾娥达的年轻寡妇，正被带往一场会夺去她生命的仪式。
She was unable to choose freely, and the travelers realized that she needed help at once.|她无法自由选择，旅行者们意识到，她需要立即得到帮助。
Fogg still had time in his schedule, and he decided to use it to try to save her.|福格的行程中还有余量，他决定利用这段时间尝试救人。
Their first attempt failed, but Passepartout found another chance by using his courage and skill as a performer.|他们第一次尝试失败了，路路通却凭借勇气和表演本领找到了另一个机会。
He secretly took the place of the dead husband, then rose and carried Aouda away while the watchers fled in fear.|他悄悄替换了死去的丈夫，随后起身抱走艾娥达，围观者则惊恐地逃散。
With their guide's help, the party escaped and continued toward the railway.|在向导帮助下，一行人脱身，继续赶往铁路。
Aouda traveled with them, and the race around the world now included a responsibility that no timetable had listed.|艾娥达与他们同行，而这场环球竞赛也多了一份时刻表从未列出的责任。
## Trouble Before the Ship|开船之前的麻烦
At Calcutta, another delay waited for them, this time inside a court.|加尔各答又有一场耽搁等着他们，这次是在法庭里。
Earlier in Bombay, Passepartout had entered a temple without following its rules, and the matter had followed him here.|先前在孟买，路路通进入一座寺庙时没有遵守规定，这件事一路追到了这里。
Fix hoped the case would keep Fogg in place until the bank's arrest warrant arrived.|费克斯希望此案能拖住福格，直到银行盗案的逮捕令送到。
But Fogg paid the required bail, and the travelers reached their ship in time.|不过福格支付了要求的保释金，一行人及时赶上轮船。
They sailed from Calcutta toward Hong Kong by way of Singapore.|他们从加尔各答出发，经新加坡驶向香港。
Aouda hoped to find a relative in Hong Kong, while Fogg continued checking the next connection.|艾娥达希望在香港找到一位亲戚，福格则继续核对下一段交通安排。
Fix sailed with them, convinced that every mile was carrying a criminal farther from justice.|费克斯也一同航行，深信每走一英里，都是让一个罪犯离正义更远一步。
## A Secret in Hong Kong|香港的秘密
In Hong Kong, Aouda learned that her relative had moved to Europe, so Fogg offered to take her there with them.|在香港，艾娥达得知亲戚已迁往欧洲，福格便提出让她继续同行。
Meanwhile, Passepartout discovered that their next ship would leave earlier than expected.|与此同时，路路通发现下一班船会比预期更早离港。
Before he could warn Fogg, Fix drew him into a private conversation and revealed that he was a detective.|他还没来得及通知福格，费克斯就把他带到一旁谈话，透露了自己的侦探身份。
Passepartout refused to help arrest his master, whose kindness he had seen for himself.|路路通亲眼见过主人的善意，拒绝协助逮捕他。
Fix then used drink and a drug to leave him unable to carry the warning back.|费克斯随后利用酒和毒品，使路路通无法回去传递消息。
The servant missed his chance to explain the changed departure, and the travelers were separated.|仆人错过了说明开船时间变化的机会，几位旅行者因此失散。
Fogg and Aouda waited in vain for Passepartout, while the ship they needed prepared to sail without them.|福格和艾娥达徒劳地等着路路通，而他们需要搭乘的船，已准备在没有他们的情况下起航。
## A Small Boat and a Storm|小船与风暴
Passepartout recovered enough to reach the departing ship, but Fogg and Aouda were not aboard.|路路通恢复过来，赶上了即将离港的船，可福格和艾娥达并不在船上。
They had to find another route, and Fogg hired a small sailing boat called the Tankadere.|他们必须另找路线，于是福格租下一艘名叫坦卡德尔号的小帆船。
Its captain could not take them all the way to Japan, but he could try to reach Shanghai.|船长无法把他们一路送到日本，但可以尝试赶到上海。
There they might meet the larger steamship traveling onward to Yokohama and across the Pacific.|在那里，他们也许能赶上继续前往横滨、再横渡太平洋的大轮船。
Fix joined the small boat, while its crew fought a fierce storm that made speed and safety difficult to balance.|费克斯也上了小船；船员们与猛烈风暴搏斗，很难在速度和安全之间取得平衡。
Near their destination, they saw the steamship moving away, and the captain signaled for help by firing a gun.|临近目的地时，他们看见轮船正驶离，于是船长鸣枪求助。
The signal was noticed, and Fogg's party gained another chance to continue the journey.|信号被发现了，福格一行又获得一次继续旅程的机会。
## The Performer in Japan|日本的表演者
Passepartout arrived in Yokohama alone, with little money and no idea where his companions were.|路路通独自抵达横滨，钱所剩无几，也不知道同伴们在哪里。
He walked through unfamiliar streets, searching for food and a way to earn his passage onward.|他穿过陌生街道，寻找食物，也寻找挣到下一段船费的办法。
His old performance skills helped him find work with a troupe of acrobats.|过去的表演本领，帮助他在一个杂技团找到了工作。
During a show, he took his place in a tall human tower, wearing part of a strange costume.|一次演出中，他穿着奇特服装，在高高的人塔上站好自己的位置。
Then he saw Fogg and Aouda in the audience, and excitement swept away every thought of the performance.|突然，他看见福格和艾娥达就在观众中，激动之下，把演出的事忘得一干二净。
He rushed toward them, bringing the tower down and ending his short career with the troupe.|他向他们冲去，弄倒了人塔，也结束了自己在团里短暂的工作。
The travelers were together again, and soon they boarded the steamship bound for San Francisco.|旅行者们终于重聚，不久便登上了驶往旧金山的轮船。
## Across the Pacific|横渡太平洋
The Pacific stretched around their ship, wider than any ocean passage they had crossed so far.|太平洋在轮船四周展开，比他们此前经过的任何海洋航段都更辽阔。
Fogg studied his schedule, Passepartout looked after his duties, and Aouda grew increasingly fond of their quiet companion.|福格研究行程，路路通照料事务，艾娥达则越来越喜欢这位沉静的同行者。
Fix had obtained the warrant, but outside British territory he could not use it as he wished.|费克斯拿到了逮捕令，可在英国领地之外，他无法按自己的意愿使用它。
Now he wanted Fogg to reach Britain quickly, where he planned to make the arrest.|现在他反倒希望福格尽快回到英国，以便实施逮捕。
Passepartout learned that Fix would help them onward, though the two men had very different reasons for continuing.|路路通得知费克斯会帮助他们继续前进，尽管两个人这样做的理由完全不同。
After the long crossing, the ship reached San Francisco, and the travelers hurried toward a train across America.|漫长航行之后，轮船抵达旧金山，旅行者们匆忙赶往横穿美国的火车。
Their route was still moving east, from one coast toward another and from one set of difficulties to the next.|路线仍在向东延伸，从一个海岸通往另一个海岸，也从一组难题走向下一组难题。
## Rails Across America|横穿美国的铁轨
The train carried them through great open spaces, past towns, mountains, and plains.|列车载着他们驶过辽阔大地，经过城镇、山脉和平原。
Once, a huge herd of bison blocked the line, and the powerful engine had to wait for living traffic to pass.|有一次，庞大的野牛群挡住铁路，强大的火车头也只能等待这些活生生的路障通过。
Later, a damaged bridge threatened another delay, and the crew decided to rush the train across it at speed.|后来，一座损坏的桥又可能造成耽搁，乘务人员决定让火车快速冲过。
Passepartout thought the passengers should cross safely first, but events moved too quickly for his suggestion.|路路通认为应该让乘客先安全过去，可事情发展太快，他的建议没来得及实施。
The train reached the far side, and the bridge fell behind it.|火车到了对岸，大桥随即在身后坍塌。
They had escaped, but a successful outcome did not make the choice free from danger.|他们脱险了，可结果成功，并不意味着当时的选择没有危险。
Fogg's timetable kept its neat columns while the real journey shook, stopped, and changed beneath their feet.|福格的时刻表仍然排列整齐，真实的旅程却在脚下颠簸、停顿，不断变化。
## The Train Without a Driver|失控的列车
Farther along the line, a group of Sioux riders attacked the train, and fighting broke out among the moving carriages.|在更远的路段，一群苏族骑手袭击了列车，行驶的车厢间爆发了战斗。
The engine driver was struck down, and the train continued without anyone properly controlling its speed.|火车司机受伤倒下，列车在无人正常控制速度的情况下继续前进。
Passepartout climbed beneath the carriages and managed to separate them from the engine.|路路通爬到车厢下方，设法将车厢与火车头分开。
The carriages slowed near a fort, where soldiers came to help.|车厢在一座堡垒附近慢下来，士兵们赶来救援。
But when the attack ended, Passepartout and several other passengers were missing.|然而袭击结束后，路路通和另外几名乘客失踪了。
Fogg could continue without his servant, or he could lose precious time trying to bring him back.|福格可以抛下仆人继续赶路，也可以花掉宝贵时间，设法把他带回来。
He chose the rescue and left with a party of soldiers while Aouda waited anxiously.|他选择了营救，与一队士兵出发，艾娥达则焦急地等待。
## A Sail over Snow|雪地上的风帆
The rescue party returned with the missing travelers, but the train had already departed.|救援队带着失踪旅客回来了，可火车早已开走。
Fogg had kept his friend, yet the lost hours now threatened the entire journey.|福格救回了朋友，但失去的几个小时，却威胁着整趟旅程。
Fix helped arrange another strange form of transport: a sledge fitted with sails.|费克斯帮助安排了另一种奇特交通工具：装着风帆的雪橇。
A strong wind pushed it over the frozen plain, carrying the travelers toward a place where they could catch another train.|强风推动雪橇滑过冰冻平原，把旅行者们送往能搭上另一班火车的地方。
Wrapped against the bitter cold, they watched the white ground rush beneath them.|他们裹紧衣物抵御严寒，看着白色地面在身下飞快掠过。
The sledge and the next train brought them to New York, but not soon enough for their planned Atlantic steamer.|雪橇和下一班火车把他们送到了纽约，却没能让他们赶上计划中的大西洋轮船。
The ship was gone, and once again Fogg stood at a waterfront with an ocean between him and home.|轮船已经离开，福格又一次站在码头，家与自己之间隔着一片大洋。
## A Different Atlantic Crossing|另一条大西洋航线
Fogg searched the harbor and found the Henrietta, a ship whose captain intended to sail to Bordeaux in France.|福格在港口寻找，找到亨利埃塔号，船长原计划前往法国波尔多。
The captain refused to change his destination, but he agreed to carry the travelers as passengers.|船长拒绝更改目的地，却同意载上他们。
Once at sea, Fogg persuaded the crew to support him, confined the captain, and directed the ship toward Britain.|出海后，福格说服船员支持自己，把船长关了起来，并让船驶向英国。
It was a bold and troubling act, not simply another ticket bought at a station.|这是一个大胆而令人不安的行为，并不等于在车站多买了一张票。
The ship's new course took them toward home, with every day of the crossing eating into their remaining time.|轮船的新航向把他们带向家乡，而航行每过一天，剩下的时间就减少一天。
Fogg could change the course of a ship more easily than he could change the weather or the distance across the ocean.|福格可以改变船的航向，却无法同样轻易地改变天气，或缩短横跨大洋的距离。
Ahead lay Britain, but first they had to make the vessel keep moving.|英国就在前方，但首先，他们必须让船持续前进。
## Wood for the Furnace|送进炉膛的木材
Then the coal supply ran low, and reaching the coast became a problem of fuel as well as time.|接着，煤炭供应不足，能否抵达海岸，既取决于时间，也取决于燃料。
The engine needed heat, and heat required something to burn.|发动机需要热量，而产生热量就需要燃烧的东西。
Fogg bargained with the captain and bought the ship, leaving him the engine and metal hull.|福格与船长商议，买下船，并把发动机和金属船壳留给他。
Now the crew could remove wooden parts of the vessel and feed them into the furnace.|现在，船员们可以拆下船上的木制部分，送进炉膛。
The fuel was coming from the ship itself, so each extra mile had a cost that everyone could see.|燃料来自船身本身，因此每多走一英里，大家都看得见它的代价。
This could not continue forever, but it might provide enough power to reach the coast.|这种办法无法永远持续，却可能提供足够动力，让船抵达海岸。
As the damaged ship pressed onward, Fogg watched for the next chance to shorten the route home.|被拆损的轮船继续前进，福格则寻找下一个能缩短归程的机会。
## The Arrest at Liverpool|利物浦的逮捕
Near Ireland, Fogg used a faster mail route, landing at Queenstown and taking a train to Dublin.|抵近爱尔兰时，福格利用更快的邮递路线，在皇后镇上岸，乘火车前往都柏林。
A fast boat then carried the party to Liverpool, bringing London within reach at last.|随后，一艘快船把一行人送到利物浦，伦敦终于近在可及之处。
At that moment, Fix stepped forward and arrested Fogg for the bank robbery.|就在这时，费克斯走上前，以银行盗案为由逮捕福格。
The traveler who had crossed oceans and continents was stopped inside a room, unable to continue.|这位穿过大洋和大陆的旅行者，被关在房间里，无法继续赶路。
Later, Fix returned with terrible news for his own theory: the real thief had already been caught.|后来费克斯回来，带来一个推翻自己判断的消息：真正的盗贼已经被抓住了。
Fogg was released and arranged a special train, but he reached London five minutes after the time he believed was his deadline.|福格获释后安排了专列，却在自己认为的截止时间过去五分钟后，才抵达伦敦。
After so many narrow escapes, he went home believing that he had lost.|经历无数次险险赶上之后，他回到家中，认定自己输了。
## What the Journey Was Worth|旅程的价值
The next day, Fogg stayed at home, thinking that the wager and much of his fortune were gone.|第二天，福格留在家里，以为自己输掉了赌约，也失去了大部分财产。
Passepartout blamed himself for the delays, while Aouda thought about everything Fogg had done for her.|路路通为种种耽搁责怪自己，艾娥达则想着福格为她做过的一切。
She asked whether he would marry her, offering companionship when he thought he had little left to offer anyone.|她问他是否愿意娶自己，在他觉得已没什么可给予别人时，向他许下一生相伴。
Fogg loved her too, and their conversation changed the meaning of what he had brought home.|福格也爱着她，这段谈话让他重新理解，自己究竟从旅途中带回了什么。
They sent Passepartout to ask a clergyman about arranging the wedding for the following day.|他们让路路通去找牧师，询问能否在次日举行婚礼。
A little later, the servant burst back into the house, breathless and almost unable to speak.|不久，仆人喘着气冲回屋里，几乎说不出话。
The mistake he had discovered was not about a train or a ship, but about the day itself.|他发现的错误不在火车，也不在轮船，而在日期本身。
## One More Day|多出来的一天
They had returned on Friday, not Saturday, and the evening they now called Sunday was really Saturday.|他们回来那天是星期五，而非星期六；此刻他们以为是星期日的傍晚，其实是星期六。
Traveling east around the earth had given their count of days one extra sunrise compared with the people who stayed in London.|绕地球向东旅行，让他们计算的日子比一直留在伦敦的人多出了一次日出。
They had not traveled into the past or made more hours; they had failed to correct their calendar during the journey.|他们并没有回到过去，也没有创造更多小时，只是在旅途中没有正确调整日历。
Fogg still had a chance to win, but now only minutes remained before the real deadline.|福格仍有机会获胜，可距离真正的截止时间只剩几分钟了。
He hurried to the club and arrived just in time, astonishing the men who had been waiting there.|他匆忙赶往俱乐部，及时抵达，让等在那里的先生们大吃一惊。
The wager was won, though the journey had cost nearly as much as the prize.|赌约赢了，尽管旅费几乎花掉了与奖金同样多的钱。

## After the Journey|旅程之后
Around the World in Eighty Days is an adventure novel about travel in the nineteenth century, not a modern travel guide.|《八十天环游地球》是一部讲述十九世纪旅行的冒险小说，不是现代旅行指南。
Its famous trip uses trains, ships, an elephant, and a sailing sledge, but the travelers do not fly around the world in a balloon.|这趟著名旅程使用火车、轮船、大象和风帆雪橇，但旅行者们并没有乘气球环游世界。
Today, crossing the International Date Line requires a calendar change: going east means moving the date back one day.|今天，越过国际日期变更线需要调整日历：向东越过时，日期减去一天。
That rule helps people keep local dates consistent after a journey around the earth.|这条规则帮助人们在绕地球旅行后，让当地日期保持一致。
The novel also reflects old prejudices about different peoples, so its descriptions should not be treated as a complete picture of those places.|小说也反映了对不同人群的旧有偏见，因此不能把其中的描写当作那些地方的完整面貌。
