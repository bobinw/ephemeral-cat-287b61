Greenfield: Building a City

A City Made of Blocks
Greenfield is a city inside Minecraft. It is a building project made by a team of real players. The team takes ideas from Los Angeles and the west coast of America. Their aim is a city that looks like a place where people could really live.

More Than One Building
The project's official wiki describes a team of more than 150 people. It lists districts, landmarks, buildings, and transport. A city is more than one beautiful building. It is many places connected together. This makes a city project different from building one house.

A Shared World
A team project raises interesting questions. If your building looks wonderful alone, will it fit beside someone else's building? Who decides where a road should go?

Your City
Imagine joining a city-building team. What would you add first: a harbor, a school, a park, or a station? How would your choice help other parts of the city? A shared world invites you to think about both your own idea and the needs of others.
