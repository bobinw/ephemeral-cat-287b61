# 神秘岛 · 原作探险版
## A Balloon in the Storm|风暴中的气球
Over the Pacific Ocean, a damaged balloon was falling towards the waves.|太平洋上空，一只受损的气球正向海浪坠落。
Five travellers threw out their supplies to make it lighter, but gas still escaped through the torn cloth.|五位旅行者把物资扔出去，试着减轻重量，可气体仍从破布中漏出。
Only a few days earlier, they had escaped from Richmond during the American Civil War.|就在几天前，他们趁着美国内战中的混乱，从里士满逃了出来。
The storm had carried them far beyond any route they could choose or follow.|风暴把他们带到了遥远的地方，他们既无法选择路线，也无法追踪航向。
Cyrus Smith, an engineer, tried to save what remained of the balloon.|工程师赛勒斯·史密斯设法挽救气球剩下的部分。
Gideon Spilett was a reporter, used to watching carefully even when danger was close.|吉迪恩·斯皮莱是一名记者，即使危险近在眼前，也习惯仔细观察。
The sailor Pencroft worked beside Herbert, a young companion who loved plants and animals.|水手潘克洛夫和年轻同伴赫伯特一起忙碌着；赫伯特喜爱植物和动物。
Neb, Smith's devoted friend, was with them, and so was the dog Top.|史密斯忠实的朋友纳布也在，还有狗托普。
When land finally appeared, they cut away the heavy basket and clung to the net above it.|陆地终于出现时，他们割掉沉重的吊篮，紧紧抓住上方的网。
The balloon struck the water, rose once more, and dragged them towards a strange shore.|气球撞上海水，又升起一次，把他们拖向一片陌生的海岸。

## One Friend Missing|少了一位朋友
Four men reached the land, exhausted and soaked; Smith and Top had vanished into the sea.|四个人筋疲力尽、浑身湿透地到了岸上；史密斯和托普却消失在海里。
Neb searched the waves, refusing to believe that the engineer was gone for ever.|纳布搜索着海面，不肯相信工程师会永远离去。
He had once been enslaved, and Smith had helped him gain his freedom.|他曾被迫为奴，史密斯帮助他获得了自由。
Now Neb stayed beside him by choice, and nothing could persuade him to stop looking.|如今，纳布出于自己的意愿留在他身边，谁也劝不动他放弃寻找。
While Neb and Spilett searched, Pencroft and Herbert found shelter among hollow rocks near a river.|纳布和斯皮莱寻找同伴时，潘克洛夫与赫伯特在河边的空心岩石间找到了庇护所。
They called the place the Chimneys because the narrow passages reminded them of stone chimneys.|他们把这里叫作“烟囱”，因为狭窄的通道像石头烟道。
A single match, found in the lining of Spilett's clothes, gave them their first small fire.|在斯皮莱衣服衬里找到的一根火柴，给了他们第一小堆火。
Later, Top returned alone and led them through the darkness to a distant shelter.|后来，托普独自回来了，引着他们穿过黑暗，来到远处的藏身处。
There they found Smith alive, but too weak to explain how he had arrived.|他们在那里找到了还活着的史密斯，可他太虚弱，无法说明自己是怎样到达的。
He remembered the water, not the journey from the sea; the island had already given them a mystery.|他记得海水，却不记得从海里来到这里的过程；这座岛已经给了他们第一个谜。

## Borrowing Fire from the Sun|向太阳借火
The engineer slowly recovered, while the others brought food and tried to keep him warm.|工程师慢慢恢复，其他人给他送来食物，设法让他保持温暖。
But their shelter at the Chimneys had suffered in rough weather, and their precious fire was lost.|可“烟囱”庇护所在恶劣天气中受了损，那堆宝贵的火也熄灭了。
There were no more matches, and Pencroft found that rubbing wood was harder than telling stories about it.|他们再没有火柴了，而潘克洛夫发现，摩擦木头生火可比讲这种故事难多了。
Smith looked at the few things they still possessed, including two watches.|史密斯看了看他们仅剩的东西，其中包括两只怀表。
He used their curved glass covers with water between them to make a simple lens.|他用两只弯曲的表蒙，中间夹上水，做成一片简单的透镜。
The lens gathered sunlight into a bright point, and at last a little smoke appeared.|透镜把阳光聚成明亮的一点，终于，一缕轻烟出现了。
Pencroft had expected a miracle; instead he saw an idea put to work.|潘克洛夫原以为需要奇迹，结果却看见一个想法变成了实际办法。
It did not give them a factory, a ship, or a road home, but it gave them heat and hope.|这没有给他们工厂、船只或回家的路，却给了他们温暖和希望。
Smith could not make supplies appear from nowhere, yet he could help them see new uses for ordinary things.|史密斯不能凭空变出物资，却能帮助大家发现普通东西的新用途。
Before sleeping, they made sure the fire would be carefully watched.|睡觉前，他们安排好人，小心看守这堆火。

## An Island Beneath Their Feet|脚下原来是一座岛
Once Smith could travel, they climbed the mountain rising above the forests.|史密斯能够行走后，他们登上了高出森林的山峰。
From its summit they saw sea on every side, with no mainland waiting beyond the next valley.|从山顶望去，四面都是海，没有哪片大陆藏在下一个山谷后面。
Their new home was an island, and no ship could be seen on the horizon.|他们的新家是一座岛，地平线上看不见一艘船。
They named it Lincoln Island, after the American president, and called the mountain Mount Franklin.|他们以美国总统的名字，把它命名为林肯岛，又把山叫作富兰克林山。
Together they studied the coastline, the river, the lake, and the paths through the trees.|他们一起研究海岸、河流、湖泊，以及穿过树林的道路。
Herbert noticed living things; Pencroft searched for useful wood and places where a boat might land.|赫伯特留意生物；潘克洛夫寻找可用的木材，以及适合船只靠岸的地方。
Smith thought about distances, heights, water, and the materials hidden in the ground.|史密斯思考距离、高度、水源，以及地下藏着的材料。
Spilett wrote things down so that a useful observation would not be forgotten.|斯皮莱把事情记下来，让有用的观察不会被忘掉。
Knowing the island could not rescue them by itself, but it could help them plan their next step.|了解这座岛本身不能让他们获救，却能帮助他们计划下一步。
They returned to the shore with a map in their minds and a clearer idea of the work ahead.|他们带着心中的地图回到海岸，也更清楚接下来需要做什么。

## Tools Before Comfort|先造工具，再谈舒适
Pencroft wanted strong tools, warm clothes, a fine house, and a boat big enough to leave.|潘克洛夫想要结实的工具、暖和的衣服、好房子，以及一艘足以离岛的船。
The island offered none of these ready-made, so their first problem was where to begin.|岛上没有哪一样现成的东西，因此，他们的第一个问题是从哪里开始。
Smith chose small beginnings: clay, stone, wood, and the heat of their fire.|史密斯选择从小事开始：黏土、石头、木材，还有炉火的热量。
They shaped clay into bricks and pots, then learned how to make them useful through firing.|他们把黏土做成砖块和陶罐，再学习怎样通过烧制让它们派上用场。
A cooking pot changed their meals, because food could now be boiled instead of only roasted.|一只陶锅改变了他们的饭菜，因为食物除了烤，还可以煮了。
Later, they worked iron from material found on the island and made stronger tools.|后来，他们从岛上的材料中炼出铁，做成更结实的工具。
Each new tool helped make the next task possible; progress came in a chain, not in one great leap.|每一件新工具，都让下一项工作成为可能；进步是环环相扣的，不是一步登天。
Neb kept food ready, Herbert gathered knowledge, and the others shared the heavy work.|纳布准备食物，赫伯特积累知识，其他人一起承担繁重的劳动。
Their hands became sore, and many plans needed time before they produced anything useful.|他们的手磨疼了，许多计划也要花上一段时间，才能产生有用的成果。
The island was becoming familiar, but it was their work together that made it more like a home.|这座岛渐渐变得熟悉，而让它越来越像家的，是他们共同的劳动。

## A House Inside the Cliff|悬崖里面的房子
The Chimneys could shelter them for a while, but wind and waves could still threaten it.|“烟囱”可以暂时遮风，可风浪仍会威胁这里。
Smith suspected that the lake above the shore drained through a hidden passage inside the rock.|史密斯猜想，岸边高处的湖水，是通过岩石内部的一条隐秘通道流走的。
After they changed the water's course, a great hollow space became accessible inside the granite cliff.|他们改变水流方向后，花岗岩悬崖内部的一大片空洞露了出来。
They explored it carefully and found enough room for a much safer home.|他们小心探索，发现这里有足够空间，可以建成安全得多的家。
High above the beach, they made openings for light and reached the entrance by a long ladder.|在高出海滩的地方，他们开出采光口，再用长梯通往入口。
They called the place Granite House, and divided its space into rooms for different uses.|他们把这里叫作花岗岩宫，再把内部空间分成不同用途的房间。
Pencroft admired the height because enemies could not simply walk through the door.|潘克洛夫很满意这里的高度，因为敌人没法直接从门口走进来。
Neb liked having room to cook, while Herbert found endless things to observe beyond the windows.|纳布喜欢宽敞的做饭空间，而赫伯特发现窗外有看不完的东西。
When Top barked near a deep well inside the house, however, nobody could explain what troubled him.|可是，每当托普对着屋内一口深井吠叫，谁也无法解释是什么让它不安。
Even after they had built walls around their lives, there were places on the island they did not understand.|即使他们为生活筑起了屏障，岛上仍有他们不理解的地方。

## A Fortune in One Grain|一粒麦子里的财富
One day Herbert found a single grain of wheat caught in his clothing.|一天，赫伯特在衣服里发现了一粒小麦。
He almost treated it as something too small to matter, but Smith saw a beginning.|他差点以为这小东西无关紧要，史密斯却从中看到了一个开端。
One seed could produce a plant, and that plant could give them seeds for another harvest.|一粒种子能长出一株植物，这株植物又能给他们下一次收获所需的种子。
The others imagined bread at once; the engineer reminded them that the first harvest must be saved for planting.|其他人立刻想到了面包；工程师提醒他们，第一批收成必须留作播种。
They prepared a small patch of ground and protected the precious seed with great care.|他们准备了一小块土地，极其小心地保护这粒宝贵的种子。
It required rain, sunlight, soil, and patience, none of which could be replaced by wanting bread badly.|它需要雨水、阳光、土壤和耐心，而急切地想吃面包，不能代替其中任何一样。
As their fields grew, they also kept animals and stored food against the colder season.|随着田地扩大，他们也饲养动物，储存食物，准备迎接寒冷的季节。
No single person could watch every crop, mend every tool, and find every meal.|没有谁能独自照料每一片庄稼、修好每一件工具，再找齐每一餐食物。
Their small community worked because each person accepted jobs that the others depended on.|他们的小小群体能够运转，是因为每个人都承担起别人所依赖的工作。
A grain that could disappear between two fingers had changed what they expected from the future.|一粒能从两指间漏掉的麦子，改变了他们对未来的期待。

## A Bullet and a Box|子弹与木箱
During a meal, Pencroft bit into something hard and found a small bullet in the meat.|一次吃饭时，潘克洛夫咬到了硬东西，在肉里发现一颗小子弹。
The animal had survived an old wound before being caught, but who had fired the shot?|这只动物曾经中弹，却活到被他们捕获；可当初是谁开的枪？
The settlers had arrived without guns, and their searches had revealed no other home.|他们来时没有带枪，搜寻中也没有发现别人的住所。
Smith refused to turn one strange fact into an answer larger than the evidence.|史密斯不愿仅凭一件怪事，就得出超出证据所能支持的结论。
They searched more carefully, and later a large box was discovered near the coast.|他们更加仔细地搜寻，后来又在海岸附近发现了一只大箱子。
Inside were tools, weapons, books, clothes, and other supplies that seemed wonderfully suited to their needs.|箱子里有工具、武器、书籍、衣物和其他物资，竟然十分符合他们的需要。
The gift made life easier, yet it made the island's silence harder to understand.|这份礼物让生活更容易，却也让岛上的寂静更难解释。
Was it cargo from a wreck, a message from a stranger, or help placed where they would find it?|它是沉船的货物、陌生人留下的信号，还是有人故意放在他们能发现的地方的帮助？
The engineer kept the questions open instead of calling the matter solved.|工程师保留着这些问题，没有宣布事情已经解决。
They had learned to make many things for themselves, but some things clearly came from beyond their own work.|他们已经学会自己制造许多东西，可有些东西，显然并非来自他们自己的劳动。

## A Message Across the Sea|来自海上的消息
With better tools, Pencroft helped build a small sailing boat, which they called the Bonadventure.|有了更好的工具，潘克洛夫参与造出一艘小帆船，取名“乘风破浪号”。
A boat opened a new path along the coast and towards places hidden beyond their usual view.|船为他们打开了沿海岸航行的新路，也让他们能接近平日视线之外的地方。
Then a bottle brought them words from another island: a person needed help on Tabor Island.|随后，一个瓶子带来了另一座岛的消息：塔博尔岛上有人需要帮助。
It was a difficult choice, because leaving Lincoln Island meant crossing water in a very small vessel.|这个选择并不容易，因为离开林肯岛，就意味着乘小船横渡海面。
They checked the boat and supplies before Pencroft, Herbert, and Spilett set out.|他们检查好船和物资，潘克洛夫、赫伯特与斯皮莱才动身出发。
On Tabor they found signs of an abandoned home, then a lonely man who scarcely seemed able to speak.|在塔博尔岛，他们发现了废弃住所的痕迹，随后找到一个几乎已经不会说话的孤独男人。
Years without human company had deeply changed his life and behaviour.|多年没有人类同伴的生活，深深改变了他的状态与行为。
The travellers brought him back, choosing to help someone they did not yet understand.|旅行者们把他带了回去，决定先帮助这个他们还不理解的人。
On the difficult return voyage, a light helped guide them towards Lincoln Island.|在艰难的返航中，一束光帮助他们朝林肯岛驶去。
Later, another question joined the others: who had made that light shine at the right moment?|后来，又一个问题加入了谜团：是谁在恰当的时刻，让那束光亮了起来？

## Ayrton's Second Chance|艾尔通的第二次机会
The rescued man slowly regained his speech and told them that his name was Ayrton.|被救回的男人慢慢恢复了说话的能力，告诉大家，他叫艾尔通。
He had once helped criminals and betrayed people who had trusted him.|他曾经帮助罪犯，背叛过信任自己的人。
For those actions he had been left on Tabor Island, where a ship called the Duncan might one day return for him.|因为那些行为，他被留在了塔博尔岛，一艘叫“邓肯号”的船可能有一天会回来接他。
Ayrton expected judgement, and at first he found it difficult even to sit beside the others.|艾尔通以为自己会受到审判，最初，他甚至难以在别人身边坐下。
Smith and his companions did not pretend the past had never happened, but they offered him work and a place among them.|史密斯和同伴没有假装过去从未发生，却给了他工作，也让他在他们中间拥有一个位置。
They allowed trust to grow through what he did each day, rather than demanding a perfect speech.|他们让信任通过他每天的行动逐渐建立，而不是要求他说一番完美的话。
Ayrton cared for the animals at a separate enclosure and helped with the island's work.|艾尔通在一处独立的畜栏照料动物，也帮助大家在岛上干活。
Yet he said he had not written the message found in the bottle.|可他说，自己并没有写下瓶中那封求救信。
Someone had known where he was and had brought the settlers towards him.|有人知道他在哪里，并引导岛上的人找到了他。
Saving Ayrton had answered one question, while quietly opening another.|救回艾尔通回答了一个问题，却又悄悄打开了另一个问题。

## A Wire Through the Forest|穿过森林的电线
As their home grew, journeys between Granite House and the animal enclosure took more time.|随着家园发展，花岗岩宫与畜栏之间的来往越来越费时间。
Smith proposed an electric telegraph so that news could travel faster than a person on foot.|史密斯提议建一条电报线，让消息能比步行的人更快传递。
Making it required more than a clever thought: they needed wire, a source of electricity, and careful work.|要实现它，光有聪明想法还不够；他们需要电线、电源，还有细致的劳动。
The wire finally connected the two places, and signals could carry a simple message through the forest.|电线终于连起了两个地方，信号可以带着简单消息穿过森林。
Ayrton could report from the enclosure, and his companions could answer from their house in the cliff.|艾尔通能从畜栏报告情况，同伴则能从悬崖里的家回复。
Pencroft was proud of every improvement, as though they had brought a little of the wider world to their shore.|每一次改善都让潘克洛夫自豪，仿佛他们把外面的大世界带了一小片到海岸上。
But Smith still thought about the rescue, the bullet, the supplies, and the unwritten message.|可史密斯仍想着获救经过、子弹、物资，以及那封并非艾尔通所写的信。
Their own achievements were real; the unknown help did not make those achievements disappear.|他们自己的成就是真实的；未知的帮助，并没有让这些成就消失。
It did mean that the island could not be understood only by studying what the settlers themselves had done.|但这确实意味着，只研究他们自己做过的事，还不足以理解这座岛。
One day a ship appeared, and all their hopes turned towards the horizon.|一天，一艘船出现了，大家的希望都转向了地平线。

## The Black Flag|黑色的旗帜
At first they hoped the ship might carry friends, perhaps even the Duncan coming for Ayrton.|起初，他们希望船上是朋友，或许正是来接艾尔通的“邓肯号”。
Then they saw the black flag, and hope gave way to alarm.|随后，他们看见了黑旗，希望立刻变成警惕。
The ship carried dangerous men who might seize the island and everything the settlers had built.|船上是一群危险的人，可能夺走这座岛，以及大家建立的一切。
Ayrton risked his life to learn more about them, proving how far he had changed.|艾尔通冒着生命危险去探查，证明自己已经有了多大的改变。
The settlers prepared to defend their home, hiding their small number as carefully as they could.|大家准备保卫家园，尽可能不让对方知道自己人数很少。
Gunfire crossed the water, and the enemy ship's cannon threatened Granite House.|枪声越过水面，敌船的大炮威胁着花岗岩宫。
Then a sudden explosion tore through the ship, and the vessel sank before their eyes.|接着，一场突然的爆炸撕裂了船体，敌船在他们眼前沉了下去。
The settlers had not caused the explosion, and again they could not explain what had saved them.|爆炸不是他们造成的，而他们又一次无法解释，是什么救了自己。
Some of the attackers had already reached the island and escaped into the forest.|有些袭击者已经登岛，逃进了森林。
The danger had changed its shape; it had not ended with the sinking ship.|危险只是换了模样，并没有随着沉船结束。

## A Friend in Danger|朋友危在旦夕
The men hiding in the forest made even familiar paths unsafe.|躲在森林里的人，让熟悉的小路也变得不安全。
When the settlers went to the animal enclosure, a shot struck Herbert, and he fell wounded.|大家前往畜栏时，一声枪响，赫伯特中弹倒下。
Pencroft's anger vanished into fear as he looked at the young companion he loved like a son.|潘克洛夫望着这个被自己视如儿子的年轻同伴，愤怒转眼就变成了恐惧。
Spilett cared for the wound, while the others watched, waited, and guarded their shelter.|斯皮莱照料伤口，其他人守着、等着，同时保护藏身处。
Herbert began to recover, but a terrible fever later threatened his life again.|赫伯特一度开始恢复，可后来，一场可怕的高烧又威胁着他的生命。
They needed a medicine they did not possess, and all their tools seemed useless beside his bed.|他们需要一种自己没有的药，床边的所有工具此刻仿佛都失去了用处。
After a night of fear, a small box was found near him, marked with the name of the needed medicine.|经历恐惧的一夜后，人们在他身边发现一个小盒子，上面写着所需药物的名字。
Nobody in their group had placed it there, yet help had arrived when hope was almost gone.|他们之中没有谁把盒子放在那里，可帮助偏偏在希望几乎耗尽时到来了。
Herbert gradually grew stronger, and relief filled Granite House.|赫伯特渐渐强壮起来，花岗岩宫终于松了一口气。
The unseen helper was no longer only an interesting puzzle; someone had helped save their friend's life.|那个看不见的帮助者，不再只是有趣的谜题；有人帮助救回了朋友的生命。

## The Unseen Neighbour|看不见的邻居
After Herbert recovered, the settlers searched for Ayrton, who had disappeared during the troubles.|赫伯特恢复后，大家寻找在混乱中失踪的艾尔通。
They found him alive, while the remaining attackers lay dead nearby without the wounds they expected.|他们找到了还活着的他，剩余袭击者却倒在附近，身上没有他们预想的伤口。
The hidden power that had protected them was clearly capable of terrible force.|保护他们的隐秘力量，显然也能发挥可怕的威力。
Smith wanted to understand it, but he also wanted to thank whoever had shown them kindness.|史密斯想弄清真相，也想感谢那个对他们施以善意的人。
They searched caves, shores, and forest paths, yet the helper remained beyond their reach.|他们搜索洞穴、海岸和林间小路，可帮助者仍然无处可寻。
Meanwhile, Mount Franklin began to show signs that it was not as quiet as before.|与此同时，富兰克林山开始显露出迹象，不再像从前那样安静。
Smoke over a mountain was a different kind of warning, one no locked door could keep outside.|山上的烟雾是另一种警告，任何锁着的门都挡不住它。
Then the telegraph rang when nobody should have been sending a message from the enclosure.|接着，电报响了，可那时畜栏里本不该有人发消息。
A new wire led them onward, away from the route they had built with their own hands.|一根新的电线引着他们继续走，离开了那条他们亲手建起的线路。
At low tide they entered a sea cave and followed the strange light waiting within.|退潮时，他们进入海边洞穴，循着深处等待着他们的奇异光亮前进。

## The Answer Beneath the Water|水下的答案
Inside the cavern lay a long metal vessel, and within it waited an old man.|洞穴里面停着一艘长长的金属船，船里等着一位老人。
His name was Captain Nemo, and the vessel was the Nautilus.|他名叫尼摩船长，船就是“鹦鹉螺号”。
Readers of Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea may recognise both names.|读过《海底两万里》的读者，也许会认出这两个名字。
Nemo had watched the settlers for a long time, helping them without allowing them to see him.|尼摩已经观察他们很久，一直暗中帮助，却不让他们看见自己。
He had rescued Smith, left supplies, guided their boat, and brought medicine when Herbert was ill.|他救起史密斯，留下物资，引导小船，也在赫伯特生病时送来药。
The bottle leading to Ayrton and the destruction of the enemy ship were also part of his hidden work.|引导大家找到艾尔通的瓶中信，以及敌船的毁灭，也都是他的暗中行动。
Nemo told them that he had once been Prince Dakkar of India, and had fought against British rule.|尼摩告诉他们，自己曾是印度的达卡王子，反抗过英国的统治。
After terrible losses, he had left the world above and made the ocean his refuge.|经历惨重的失去后，他离开陆地上的世界，把海洋当成了避难所。
Now his companions were gone, he was old and ill, and the Nautilus could no longer carry him freely away.|如今，同伴已经不在，他也年老多病，“鹦鹉螺号”再不能载着他自由离去。
The settlers saw a generous protector, but also a lonely man whose life carried anger, grief, and difficult choices.|大家看到的是慷慨的保护者，也是一位带着愤怒、悲伤和艰难抉择走过一生的孤独老人。

## The Captain's Last Warning|船长最后的警告
The settlers remained with Nemo during his final hours, offering the company he had lived without.|在尼摩最后的时光里，大家陪在他身边，给了他久违的陪伴。
He gave them a small chest of treasure and warned Smith of a danger beneath the island.|他给了他们一小箱财宝，又提醒史密斯，岛下潜藏着危险。
The volcano and the sea were separated by rock, but that barrier would not remain safe for ever.|火山与海水之间隔着岩石，可这道屏障不会永远安全。
If water broke into the hot depths, the island could be destroyed.|如果海水冲入炽热的深处，这座岛就可能毁灭。
When Nemo died, they honoured his wish that the Nautilus should become his tomb beneath the water.|尼摩去世后，大家遵照他的愿望，让“鹦鹉螺号”成为水下的陵墓。
For the settlers, the mystery had become a person, and now that person was gone.|对他们来说，谜团已经变成了一个人，而现在，这个人又离去了。
Smith examined the threatened barrier and understood that the captain's warning could not be ignored.|史密斯查看了受到威胁的岩壁，明白船长的警告不可忽视。
They must prepare to leave the home they had worked so hard to build.|他们必须准备离开这个付出无数劳动才建成的家。
Their small boat had been destroyed during the earlier troubles, so they hurried to build another vessel.|原来的小船已在之前的混乱中毁坏，因此，他们急忙建造另一艘船。
This time they were working against a danger that would not stop to let them finish.|这一次，他们是在和一种不会停下来等他们完工的危险赛跑。

## The Night of Fire|烈火之夜
Smoke thickened above Mount Franklin, and the ground reminded them that the island was changing.|富兰克林山上方的烟越来越浓，脚下的大地不断提醒他们：这座岛正在改变。
Lava spread across places they knew, burning trees and cutting through familiar routes.|熔岩流过熟悉的地方，烧毁树木，截断曾经常走的路。
They tried to save useful supplies while continuing their work on the new ship.|他们一边抢救有用的物资，一边继续建造新船。
Fields, workshops, and paths could vanish in hours after taking months to create.|花了几个月建起的田地、工场和道路，可能在几小时内消失。
Pencroft could not bear to see their home destroyed, but anger could not turn the lava aside.|潘克洛夫不忍看家园毁灭，可愤怒没法让熔岩转向。
They hurried to make the vessel ready before the final disaster arrived.|他们拼命赶工，想在最后的灾难到来前，把船造好。
But the rock barrier failed before their work was finished, and the sea entered the burning depths.|可工程还没完成，岩石屏障就破裂了，海水涌入燃烧的深处。
A vast explosion tore Lincoln Island apart and hurled away the world they had known.|巨大的爆炸撕裂了林肯岛，也掀走了他们曾经熟悉的世界。
The six men and Top survived on a small remnant of rock above the sea.|六个人和托普活了下来，留在一小块露出海面的残岩上。
Almost everything they had made was gone, and no workshop remained from which to begin again.|他们做出的一切几乎都不在了，也再没有一间工场，可以让他们从头开始。

## A Ship Finds the Last Rock|船找到了最后的礁石
On the bare rock, the survivors had very little food and almost no protection from the weather.|在光秃秃的礁石上，幸存者们食物极少，也几乎没有抵挡风雨的地方。
Their knowledge had not disappeared, but knowledge alone could not make a ship rise from the sea.|知识并没有消失，可仅凭知识，无法让一艘船从海里升起。
At last a vessel appeared, and Ayrton recognised the Duncan coming towards them.|终于，一艘船出现了，艾尔通认出，驶来的正是“邓肯号”。
The rescuers had visited Tabor Island and found a message directing them to Lincoln Island.|救援者到过塔博尔岛，发现一封指引他们前往林肯岛的信。
Nemo had left that information there before his death, giving help that could outlive him.|尼摩在去世前就把消息留在了那里，让帮助能够延续到自己离世之后。
The last mystery ended in rescue, although the island itself could never be returned to them.|最后一个谜以获救告终，尽管那座岛再也无法回到他们身边。
They returned to America and used Nemo's gift to establish a new home in Iowa.|他们回到美国，用尼摩的赠礼在艾奥瓦州建立了新的家园。
They carried familiar names into the new landscape, keeping the memory of Lincoln Island alive.|他们把熟悉的名字带进新的土地，让林肯岛的记忆继续存在。
What had saved them included skill, patient work, friendship, and help they could never have provided alone.|拯救他们的，有技能、耐心的劳动、友谊，也有他们无法独自提供的帮助。
The island was gone, but the people who had learned to live together did not have to lose one another.|岛虽然消失了，那些学会共同生活的人，却不必再失去彼此。

## After the Adventure|探险之后
The Mysterious Island is a novel, and its island and characters belong to an imagined story.|《神秘岛》是一部小说，其中的岛屿与人物属于虚构的故事。
It draws on real ideas about plants, materials, light, electricity, and the work needed to build things.|它借用了关于植物、材料、光、电，以及建造事物所需劳动的真实知识。
But the speed of the settlers' progress and some of their inventions are simplified or exaggerated for adventure.|但为了冒险故事，岛上人们发展的速度，以及部分发明，被简化或夸张了。
Its descriptions of dangerous chemicals, weapons, medicine, and volcanic events are not instructions to copy.|其中关于危险化学物质、武器、药物和火山事件的描写，并不是可照做的操作说明。
Smith has important knowledge, yet the group also depends on Neb's care, Pencroft's skills, Spilett's judgement, and Herbert's curiosity.|史密斯拥有重要知识，但团队同样依靠纳布的照料、潘克洛夫的技能、斯皮莱的判断，以及赫伯特的好奇心。
Ayrton shows that a person's future need not be only a repetition of his worst actions.|艾尔通让我们看到，一个人的未来，不必只是最糟糕行为的不断重复。
Nemo's help is generous, but the story also gives us reasons to think about his loneliness and use of force.|尼摩的帮助十分慷慨，但故事也让我们思考他的孤独，以及他对力量的使用。
Which problem would you most like to help the settlers solve, and whose skill would you ask to learn first?|你最想帮助岛上的人解决哪个难题？又最想先向谁学习哪一种技能？
