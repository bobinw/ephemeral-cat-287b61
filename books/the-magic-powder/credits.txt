The Magic Powder - A
Folktale from Myanmar
Once upon a time, in a little village on the banks of the Irrawaddy
river, there lived a young woman called Thuza. She was very happily
married to handsome young Theingi. But there was one problem
that nagged Thuza – her husband believed he would find a way to
turn dirt into hold! Did he succeed? Read this delightful tale from
Myanmar to find out.
